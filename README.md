<h3 align="center">A progression mod for 26.2 that makes minecraft playthroughs more fun, simplest way I can explain this mod.</h3>
<h2 align="center">Features:</h2>
<div>

- Flint tools added
- Full on progression system that requires you to have the past tier to mine the new ore
- A bunch of QOL features like Mod Menu support and tooltips that tell you if you can mine that material or not
- Added a full new Amethyst tool and armor tier that's a level above diamond and is NEEDED to mine netherite.
- New endium ore with armor and tool sets (Above netherite)

</div>
<h2 align="center">Progression:</h2>
<p align="center"><strong><em>Flint > Wood > Stone > Copper > Iron > Gold > Diamond > Amethyst > Netherite</em></strong></p>
<p align="center">You must have the last tier to mine the next tier, so you cannot mine diamond with iron, you must have gold first (yes gold pickaxes can now mine diamonds)</p>
<details>
<summary align="center">First Playthrough Instructions</summary>
<p align="center">You must first collect sticks from breaking leaves on trees, then find some flint by breaking gravel (2 flint), then craft a flint knife, using that knife, start cutting down a bunch of short and tall grass to collect String, with that string, flint, and sticks, you can finally make a flint axe. Starting your playthrough.</p>
</details>
