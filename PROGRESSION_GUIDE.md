# Progression Guide

This covers how to actually progress through the mod from the very start to endium. Follow it in order or you will get stuck on certain tiers.

---

## Step 1 - Getting off the ground (no tools at all)

When you first spawn you have nothing. Punching trees does not give you wood because you need an axe first. Here is what to do:

1. Find some tall grass or short grass and break it with your hand
2. This gives you sticks sometimes, keep breaking grass until you have a few sticks
3. Find gravel and break it until you get 2 flint
4. Craft a flint knife using sticks and flint (shapeless, check the recipe book)
5. Use the flint knife to cut short grass and tall grass, this gives you string
6. With string, flint, and sticks you can now make a flint axe, pickaxe, shovel, and hoe

Once you have the flint axe you can actually chop wood and start playing normally.

---

## Step 2 - Copper

Mine some copper ore with a stone pickaxe or better. Smelt it into copper ingots. You can now craft a full copper tool and armor set. Copper is needed to be able to mine iron so do not skip it.

The progression order is: Flint > Wood > Stone > Copper > Iron > Gold > Diamond

Yes gold can mine diamond in this mod. You need gold pickaxe to get diamonds, not iron.

---

## Step 3 - Amethyst (the new tier above diamond)

Amethyst ore only generates as deepslate amethyst ore, and it is very rare. It only spawns between y -64 and y -16 and there is at most one per chunk on average. Look for it while strip mining at deep levels.

When you mine deepslate amethyst ore you get raw amethyst. To turn it into an amethyst ingot you cannot just smelt the raw amethyst directly anymore. You need to do this first:

1. Combine 1 raw amethyst + 2 amethyst shards (from vanilla amethyst geodes) in a crafting table
2. This gives you an unsmelted amethyst alloy
3. Smelt or blast the unsmelted amethyst alloy to get 1 amethyst ingot

Amethyst shards are pretty easy to find since geodes are somewhat common so the real grind is finding the raw amethyst ore.

---

## Step 4 - Diamond to Amethyst smithing

You cannot craft amethyst tools and armor directly. You need to upgrade your diamond gear using the smithing table.

To do this you need a Diamond to Amethyst Upgrade smithing template. You can find these in nether fortress chests and bastion remnant chests. They are not guaranteed so you may need to loot a few.

Once you have the template, a diamond tool or armor piece, and an amethyst ingot, use the smithing table to upgrade it. Each piece needs its own template so if you want to duplicate it:

- Put 7 diamonds + the template + 1 amethyst ingot in the crafting grid using the vanilla smithing template duplication pattern
- You get 2 templates back

Do this to get enough templates for your whole set.

---

## Step 5 - Amethyst to Netherite smithing

The vanilla diamond to netherite upgrade template no longer works. The progression now goes through amethyst first.

To upgrade amethyst gear to netherite you need the Amethyst to Netherite Upgrade template. Same deal as before, find it in nether fortress or bastion chests. To duplicate it use 7 amethyst ingots + the template + 1 netherite ingot.

Use the smithing table with the template + amethyst tool or armor + netherite ingot to get netherite gear.

---

## Step 6 - Netherite to Endium smithing

Endium ore is found in the End. It spawns on the main island and outer islands at certain height levels.

Mine it to get raw endium, smelt that into endium crystals. To craft endium tools and armor you need the Netherite to Endium Upgrade template. Find it in nether fortress or bastion chests (rarer than the other templates, roughly 10% chance per chest).

To duplicate it use 7 netherite ingots + the template + 1 endium crystal.

Use the smithing table with the template + netherite tool or armor + endium crystal to get the final endium tier.

---

## Endium Locator

Once you are in the End there is a tool called the Endium Locator. It scans loaded chunks around you and puts glowing markers on every endium ore block it finds. The markers last 50 secconds and disappear when the ore is mined.

It has 25 durability so it breaks after 25 uses. Craft it from endium materials.

---

## Quick reference

| Tier | How to unlock |
|---|---|
| Flint | Break grass for sticks, gravel for flint |
| Copper | Mine copper, smelt ingots |
| Iron | Need copper tools first |
| Gold | Need iron tools first |
| Diamond | Need gold pickaxe |
| Amethyst | Find deepslate amethyst ore (y -64 to -16), make unsmelted alloy, smelt it |
| Netherite | Find Amethyst to Netherite Upgrade template, upgrade amethyst gear |
| Endium | Find Netherite to Endium Upgrade template, upgrade netherite gear |

---

## Template drop locations

All 3 upgrade templates can drop from:
- Nether Fortress chests
- Bastion Remnant chests (other, bridge, hoglin stable, treasure)

Note that the netherite upgrade template from vanilla no longer drops from bastion treasure.
